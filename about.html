About De Bril

Redefining sophistication, one frame at a time.

De Bril Eyewear brings together designers, artisans and optical experts who share one passion: frames that move past trends and stand the test of time.

Every face is unique. Every frame should be too.

We offer an extensive collection of exclusive designs, curated to suit different face shapes and personal preferences, from a classic silhouette to a daring statement piece.

Every frame is imported and selected for De Bril, then fitted close to home by partner opticians across Kerala.
